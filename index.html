GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

Is this the official PENSA UDS-NYC fundraising?

Yes. This page is published by the PENSA UDS-NYC media team for Mega Fundraising ’26. You can confirm any detail with the executives listed in the contact section below.

What will the money be used for?

Contributions go toward purchasing musical instruments and equipment for PENSA UDS-NYC, to support ministry and worship.

How do I give?

Use MTN Mobile Money. Send to the MoMo number or merchant ID shown in the contribution card, use the payment reference megafundraising, and check that the account name reads PENTECOST STUDENTS AND ASSOCIATES before you confirm. This website does not process payments.

Do I have to attend to give?

No. You can give from anywhere using the MoMo details, and you are also welcome to join us at PENSA Auditorium.

Can I pledge now and give later?

Yes. Use the “Make a pledge” button in the contribution section to tell us how much you plan to give and when.

How will I know my gift was received?

Keep the MoMo confirmation message from your transaction. The progress bar on this page is updated once the organising team has confirmed contributions. If you need to check on a specific gift, contact the Financial Secretary.

When and where is it happening?

14th to 18th October 2026 at PENSA Auditorium. Wednesday, Thursday and Friday sessions start at 6:30 PM, and Sunday’s session starts at 7:00 AM. There is no session on Saturday, 17th October.

Is there a minimum amount?

Every contribution, large or small, is welcome and helps us move closer to the goal.
